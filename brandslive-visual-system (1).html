02 · Logo

The mark & lockup

Use the official lockup from the brand kit. The placeholder below shows construction rules — clear space, minimum size, and placement.

b Brandslive
  • Clear space Keep padding equal to the height of the “b” mark on all sides.
  • Min size 24px tall (digital) / 12mm (print). Below this, use the mark only.
  • Versions Full-colour, all-white (on colour/photo), all-ink (light backgrounds).
  • Placement Top-left or bottom-left of a banner — never centre unless it’s the sole hero element.
  • Don’t Recolour, stretch, add shadows, rotate, or place on a busy area without a scrim.

    08 · Rules

    Do & Don't

    ✓ Always

  • ✅ Lead with one benefit, in plain Hinglish
  • ✅ Keep one CTA in saffron per banner
  • ✅ Show the real app on a phone
  • ✅ Respect the safe zone & 8-pt spacing
  • ✅ Build editable masters for language swaps
  • ✅ Use the 4-power icons consistently

    • ✕ Never

  • 🚫 Cram multiple offers/CTAs on one banner
  • 🚫 Use more than 2 type weights or tiny body text
  • 🚫 Recolour or distort the logo
  • 🚫 Fill every pixel — chaos kills trust
  • 🚫 Use generic corporate stock photography
  • 🚫 Put text in story/reel UI overlap zones
